\section{Related work}\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Citation of retracted papers.} Retracted papers continue to be cited after retraction, often without
acknowledging it \citep{hsiao2021continued,schneider2020continued}; see \citet{teixeira2025citation} for a review. Attention to
retracted papers and its decline after retraction have been measured across platforms \citep{peng2022attention} and
related to how widely known a finding was \citep{memon2025widely}. Large open resources now document post-retraction
citations in biomedicine, including citation contexts \citep{hsiao2021continued,joseph2026sanecite}. Case studies
traced how far a single retracted result spreads through direct and indirect citations
\citep{vandervet2016propagation}, and harm has been quantified by citation distance from retracted work
\citep{harm2025dynamics}.

\paragraph{Retractions in citation networks.} In molecular biology, retracted papers form citation communities with
high self-citation \citep{feng2024molbio}. Paper-mill retractions reference and are cited by other later-retracted
papers \citep{candal2024references}. Across Web of Science, retractions cluster in citation networks, and citing
retracted works is associated with higher subsequent retraction rates \citep{lou2026beyond}. These studies are
retrospective and do not separate the network signal from journal-level clustering.

\paragraph{Predicting retraction.} Classifiers trained on titles, abstracts and metadata reach accuracies around 0.6--0.7
on balanced samples, with a random split \citep{fletcher2025predicting}. The Problematic Paper Screener flags papers
that cite retracted work for reassessment \citep{cabanac2022pps}; how well such flags anticipate later retractions has
not been reported.

\paragraph{Funding.} Misconduct-related retractions of NIH-funded papers carry direct costs \citep{stern2014costs}, and
retractions are followed by less funding and fewer citations in the affected fields \citep{azoulay2015retractions}.
How often NIH-funded papers themselves cite retracted work has not, to my knowledge, been measured.

\paragraph{Open citation data.} OpenCitations provides open citation links and metadata
\citep{peroni2020opencitations,heibi2019coci,massari2024meta}; its coverage differs from commercial indexes
\citep{martinmartin2021coverage}.
